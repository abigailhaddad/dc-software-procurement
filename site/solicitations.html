Solicitations

What DC is about to buy. A solicitation is the District's public request for bids, quotes or proposals. First the ones open when we pulled, soonest closing first; then every solicitation back to fiscal year 2013, searchable and filterable. The full documents are on DC's solicitations search.

What this covers

A solicitation is an RFP (request for proposals), RFQ, IFB (invitation for bids) or RFI (request for information, which only asks questions). RFQ can mean a request for quotations or a request for qualifications, which asks only for vendors' qualifications. Here it mostly means quotations: titles that spell it out say quotation or quote about three times as often as qualifications. DC's site lists each one with a title, agency, market type, closing date and the contract specialist to contact. It publishes no description, posting date, dollar value or commodity code, and doesn't say which contract a solicitation led to. Every value here is as the District entered it.
