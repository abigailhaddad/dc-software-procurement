DC procurement data

What the District of Columbia's Contracts and Procurement Transparency Portal publishes, in tables you can filter, total and check.

Contracts About this data

One row per contract option period: a contract with a base period and two option years is three rows. Each row's amount is what that period authorises the District to spend, not what was paid. Every field below is the District's, as published: agency, vendor, contract type, market type and commodity code are what was entered on the portal, not categories added here.

Break it down

Group the filtered rows by one or two fields. Every value is as the District entered it.

The rows

Data: contracts.ocp.dc.gov, archived weekly. Queries run in your browser with DuckDB; nothing you filter is sent anywhere.
