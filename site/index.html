DC procurement data

A copy of the records on contracts.ocp.dc.gov, the website where DC's Office of Contracting and Procurement publishes contracts, purchase orders and payments, as we pulled them. About this source. Not a complete record of District contracting or spending.

Contracts Payments Purchase orders About this data

One row per contract option period: a contract with a base period and two option years is three rows. Each row's amount is what that period authorises the District to spend, not what was paid. Every field below is the District's, as published: agency, vendor, contract type, market type and commodity code are what was entered on DC's site, not categories added here.
