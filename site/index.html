New here? Start with the questions this site can answer, such as what technology DC buys and what comes up for renewal.

One row per contract option period: a contract with a base period and two option years is three rows. Only base-period amounts are shown and totalled. They are what the first period authorises the District to spend, not what was paid. Option-period amounts are left out because about three in ten we checked disagreed with the District's own paperwork (how we know). Every other field below is the District's, as published: agency, vendor, contract type, market type and commodity code are as entered on DC's site, not categories added here.
