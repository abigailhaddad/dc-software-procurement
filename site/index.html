Start here

The contracts, purchase orders, payments and solicitations DC lists on its Contracts and Procurement Transparency Portal (contracts.ocp.dc.gov), copied weekly since August 2026. Pick a question.

Contract amounts here are what a contract's first period lets DC spend, not what it paid. Some agencies list few or none of their contracts on the portal. See what the portal leaves out before quoting a total.

  • What technology does DC buy, and from whom?

    Contracts and purchase orders under DC's computer, software, IT-service and network commodity codes, by agency, vendor and year. The codes miss some software; the page measures how much.

  • What comes up for renewal or re-bid soon?

    Contracts whose current period ends in the next 6, 12 or 24 months. DC's site doesn't say how many option years are left, so for most contracts that is marked "Not known".

    Ending within 6 months · Technology, within 12 months

  • What is DC about to buy?

    Open solicitations, soonest closing first, and every solicitation back to fiscal year 2013. DC's site doesn't link a solicitation to the contract it became.

    Titles containing "software"

  • Which vendors sell to many agencies?

    Vendors with contracts or orders at several agencies, and a search for one product across agencies. Names are as DC spells them, and a reseller's name covers many makers' products.

    Technology vendors at 5+ agencies

  • What wasn't competed?

    Contracts whose paperwork shows a sole-source, emergency, exempt, letter-contract, special-pilot or rider (cooperative) award, or an extension past the last option. We read the paperwork only for current contracts whose attached file names mention a sole source, emergency, letter contract or extension, or whose record looked unusual. Others awarded the same way may not be on the list.

    Sole-source contracts · What each label means

  • How is DC supposed to buy?

    Buying authority, OCTO's review of technology purchases, when the Council must approve a contract, small-business set-asides and contract types, each with its DC Code or DCMR section.

  • What can't this data tell me?

    Agencies outside OCP's authority, payments with no contract number, option-period amounts that disagree with the paperwork, records the search drops and fields left blank, each with a number.

  • I want the records themselves

    Every contract option period, purchase order and payment line, with filters, charts and CSV downloads.
