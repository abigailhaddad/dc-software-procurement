About this data

Every table on this site is the District's own record from its Contracts and Procurement Transparency Portal, pulled weekly and kept in a public archive. Fields are shown as the District entered them. Nothing is classified or estimated. The one thing filled in is a short list of agency names published only as an abbreviation or a budget code, listed below with a source for each.

Things to know before you total anything

Agency names filled in

The value as published is kept beside the name shown in every table, as agency_as_published. Values with no source, such as ELC, are left as published. Abbreviations are never merged on their own: two different agencies publish as OPC.

What each column is

Columns ending in _text are exactly as published; the numbers and dates beside them are parsed from those.

Data: contracts.ocp.dc.gov, archived weekly.
