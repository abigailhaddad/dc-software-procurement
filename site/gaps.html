What DC's portal leaves out

What a reader can't learn, or can't learn reliably, from DC's Contracts and Procurement Transparency Portal, with a number for each and, where this site can filter to them, a link to the records behind it.

What this covers
  • Each gap describes the published records. None is a finding about how any contract was handled.
  • Every number is computed in your browser from the same tables the rest of this site uses, except where a gap says it comes from a hand-checked sample.
  • Click a gap's name for its explanation, what its number counts and its date. Each is also listed below the table.

Agencies outside OCP's authority

DC's site has a page of "Non OCP Agencies": agencies that buy under their own authority instead of the Chief Procurement Officer's. The site links out to each. Most of their contracts aren't on the contract list, though many of their purchase orders and payments are.

Other agencies with orders or payments but no contract

Payments that point to no listed contract

Each payment line has a contract number field. Most lines leave it empty, type something other than a number into it, or give a number the contract list doesn't have. Those payments can't be tied to a contract's terms, ceiling or documents.
