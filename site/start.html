Start here

This site is for anyone who needs to understand what the District buys and how, especially software and other technology. Each question below opens a page or a filtered view that answers part of it from DC's own records. Each page says what its numbers cover and what they leave out. Read what DC's site leaves out before quoting any total: some agencies publish little or nothing there, and the amounts are ceilings, not spending.

Questions

What technology does DC buy, and from whom?

Contracts and purchase orders under the District's computer, software, IT-service and network commodity codes, by agency, vendor and year. The codes are chosen when a record is entered and miss some software; the page measures how much.

Technology and software →

What comes up for renewal or re-bid soon?

Contracts whose current period ends in the next 6, 12 or 24 months. DC's site doesn't say how many option years a contract has left, so for most contracts that part is marked "Not known".

Ending within 6 months → Technology, within 12 months →

What is DC about to buy?

Open solicitations, soonest closing first, and every solicitation back to fiscal year 2013. DC's site doesn't link a solicitation to the contract it became.

Solicitations → Titles containing "software" →

Which vendors sell to many agencies?

Vendors with contracts or orders at several agencies, and a search for one product across agencies, for weighing a citywide purchase. Names are as DC spells them, and a reseller's name covers many makers' products.

Technology vendors at 5+ agencies →

What wasn't competed?

Contracts whose paperwork shows a sole-source, emergency, exempt or letter-contract award, or an extension past the last option. We read only contracts our checks flagged, so this list is incomplete.

Sole-source contracts → What each label means →

How is DC supposed to buy?

Who has buying authority, OCTO's review of technology purchases, when the Council must approve a contract, small-business set-asides, and the contract types, each with the DC Code or DCMR section it comes from.

What can't this data tell me?

Agencies outside OCP's authority, payments with no contract number, option-period amounts that disagree with the paperwork, records the search drops, and fields left blank, each with a number.
