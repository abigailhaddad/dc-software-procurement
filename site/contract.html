DC procurement data

A copy of the records on contracts.ocp.dc.gov, the website where DC's Office of Contracting and Procurement publishes contracts, purchase orders and payments, as we pulled them. About this source. Not a complete record of District contracting or spending.

Option periods

Each option period is its own record on DC's site, with its own amount: what that period authorises, not what was paid.

Payments

Paid by fiscal year

Sum of voucherAmount by the fiscal year of the payment date. Hover for the line count.

Purchase orders

An order has no contract number of its own on DC's site. These are the orders that this contract's payments were paid against: a payment line carries both a contract number and an order number. A revised order's -V suffix is ignored in matching, because the payments table leaves it off. Orders placed under this contract but never paid will not appear.

Documents

Every attachment DC's site lists on this contract's option periods, linking to the PDF on DC's site. A document filed under several option periods appears once for each.

Data: contracts.ocp.dc.gov, archived weekly. Queries run in your browser with DuckDB.
