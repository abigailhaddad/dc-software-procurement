How DC buys What DC's site leaves out About this data

Contracts whose current period ends soon. When a period ends, the District has three choices: exercise the next option (an extra year, usually, that the contract already allows), re-bid the work, or extend the contract without bidding. Which of those is possible depends on whether any options are left, and DC's site doesn't say: it lists each option period only once it has been exercised, and has no field for how many the contract allows. So the Options left column is filled in only where someone has read the contract's own paperwork; everywhere else it says "Not known".
