DC procurement data

A copy of what the District of Columbia's Contracts and Procurement Transparency Portal, the public site the Office of Contracting and Procurement runs for District contracting records, lists, as we pulled it. What the portal is. Not a complete record of District contracting or spending.

Contracts Payments Purchase orders About this data

One row per payment line, as the portal lists it, including the lines no contract page can reach: most carry no contract number, or one that is not on the portal's contract list. The amount summed is each line's voucherAmount; the portal's paymentAmount, shown as check total, is the total of the whole check run, repeated on every line in it, and is never summed. Agency, vendor and contract number are as entered.
