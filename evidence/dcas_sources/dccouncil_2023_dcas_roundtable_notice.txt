       Councilmember Christina Henderson, Chair of the Committee on Health, and
Councilmember Robert C. White, Jr., Chair of the Committee on Housing, announce a joint
oversight roundtable on Health and Human Services Benefits Enrollment and the DC Access
System (DCAS). The roundtable will be held on Thursday November 30, 2023, at 9:30a.m.

        The purpose of this roundtable is to address the challenges District residents have faced
when enrolling in health and social services benefits through DCAS. DCAS, also known as District
Direct, is a web-based application that is intended to allow District residents to apply for certain
public benefits available in the District. The goal of DCAS is to streamline the application and
recertification processes by linking a central system to various data sources to eliminate repeated
data entry through forms and applications. After more than a decade of implementation, concerns
with the performance of the software remain.

          DCAS is managed and funded under the Department of Health Care Finance (DHCF),
and benefit determinations based on the information submitted through DCAS are made by the
Department of Human Services (DHS) Economic Security Administration (ESA). This joint
oversight roundtable is intended to bring those agencies together to discus the entire benefits
enrollment processes to identify opportunities for improvement. Topics for discussion will include
the current state of the DCAS technology, staffing for processing applications, processing times
for benefits applications, the administrative burden for residents applying for benefits,
interoperability of systems and databases, plans for the future of DCAS, interactions between
DHCF and DHS for determining benefits, and plans for improving processing timelines for health
and social services benefits.
