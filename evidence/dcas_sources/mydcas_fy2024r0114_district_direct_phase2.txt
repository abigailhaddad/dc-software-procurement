RELEASE NAME      FY2024R0114 – District Direct Improvements P2
RELEASE DATE      January 14, 2024

INITIATIVE        Usability

OVERVIEW          Improvements are made to the District Direct Resident Portal and Mobile App to support the online and mobile app before the application
                  harmonization project goes live. These improvements can include accessibility language, integration with the caseworker portal, and other
                  enhancements.

BENEFITS          Users will see the following:
                  •   Release will include new requirements for Resident Portal and Mobile App
                  •   Release will include repairs to all stuck threads and error notifications

WHO’S IMPACTED?   •   Internal Stakeholders
                  •   Caseworkers​ and Beneficiaries

HOW ARE USERS     Caseworkers and other users will enjoy smoother DD resident and Mobile app processing and eligibility determination.
