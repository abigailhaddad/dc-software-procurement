Our Customers

We have delivered services to Health and Human Services agencies in various states in the United States and provinces in Canada, including the following:

ESDC, Canada

Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC) is the largest national, multi-phase Cúram implementation for the country’s social benefit programs. D2Sol provides services to multiple System Integrators (SIs) and supports the Cúram implementation.

District of Columbia

Since 2016, D2Sol has provided operations and maintenance for MAGI, SNAP, and TANF implementations within the District of Columbia Access System (DCAS). Our team merged a parallel MAGI instance with the SNAP & TANF instance into a single Cúram system, reducing maintenance and operations costs. This was a one-of-a-kind project that had never been done in the history of Cúram. Our team also implemented Non-MAGI programs with full data conversion, which went live in 2021.

State of North Carolina

D2Sol has been involved with the North Carolina Families Accessing Services through Technology (NC FAST) project since 2014, supporting the maintenance of SNAP, TANF, MAGI, and Non-MAGI programs. Our team supported the development and implementation of Child Care and Child Welfare modules, which went live in 2017. NC FAST is one of the largest Cúram implementations, and our team continues to provide maintenance and operations support.

State of South Carolina

Since 2015, D2Sol has supported the South Carolina Member Management Replacement Program (MMRP). Our team assisted in the project’s transition to IBM and worked with IBM to implement Non-MAGI Medicaid programs while maintaining the MAGI programs. We have also worked on multiple Cúram upgrades and continue to provide maintenance and operations support.

State of Minnesota

D2Sol supports the Minnesota Eligibility Technology System (METS) project with the MAGI implementation and continues to provide maintenance, operations, and Cúram upgrade activities. Our team plays a critical role in maintaining data quality and analysis, along with supporting new implementations as required.

State of New York

D2Sol has supported the New York Health Home (NYHH) project with prototyping, development, and implementation for serving the State’s aged population. Our team continues to provide maintenance and operations support for the developed system.

State of Missouri

D2Sol has supported the Missouri Eligibility Determination and Enrollment System (MEDES) project since 2014. Our team continues to support the MAGI implementation and provides ongoing maintenance and operations.
